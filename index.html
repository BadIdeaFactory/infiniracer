PROMPT

ok a few things, make the stages further apart say 10 times further apart, add any remaining time at checkpoint to the new countdown. give the player 90 seconds per stage and when you pass a checkpoint the word checkpoint! should be displayed in a cool way and then disappear.

CHANGES

  • Stages 10× further apart. Distances 6000 / 14000 / 24000 → 60 000 / 140 000 / 240 000. Stage gaps are now 60k / 80k / 100k instead of 6k / 8k / 10k
  • 90 seconds per stage across the board (was 22 / 22 / 25). With MAX_SPEED = 1600, the first stage alone takes ~37 s at full throttle — comfortable, but slow driving will burn the clock
  • Time carries over at checkpoints. When you cross a checkpoint, the leftover seconds on the current clock are added to the next stage's 90 s. So fast driving compounds — finishing stage 1 with 50 s left gives you 140 s for stage 2. Rewards committing early
  • CHECKPOINT! banner renders centre-screen on every intermediate cross. Snap-zoom from 1.4× scale to 1.0× while fading in (0.15 s), holds at full size for ~0.9 s, then fades out over 0.45 s. Two-pass text — a wide amber drop-shadow halo behind, a slightly smaller white-on-amber core in front for a vector-tube hot edge. Disappears entirely before the next stage gets going
  • CP_FLASH_DURATION = 1.5 s total. race.cpFlashT already existed as a placeholder — now wired to drawCheckpointFlash()
  • Final checkpoint (the chequered flag) does not show CHECKPOINT! — the finish overlay takes over instead
