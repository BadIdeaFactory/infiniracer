PROMPT

ok let's make a radical change. it's too easy to get a high score by simply driving slowly. add an outrun style countdown where you must complete 3 stages in a certain time, think of it as 3 checkpoints, when you hit a checkpoint before time runs out the countdown resets after the third stage you get to the chequered flag and any time you had left on the clock is generously factored into your score. maintain the style.

CHANGES

  • 3-stage race with countdown clock. Stage distances: 6000 / 14000 / 24000 units. Per-stage durations: 22 / 22 / 25 seconds. Each stage's checkpoint resets the timer to the next stage's duration. Reach the chequered flag at 24000 to finish
  • TIME OVER — when race.timeLeft hits 0, triggerCrash("timeout") fires the existing slow-mo explosion sequence with a "TIME OVER" label instead of "WIPEOUT" (separate crashReason flag distinguishes them)
  • Generous bonus at the flag — race.finalBonus = floor(timeLeft × 250,000) added on top of the base score (speed × distance at flag-cross). At MAX_SPEED a clean run can leave ~14 s on the final clock — that's a 3.5 M bonus on top of the running score
  • Top HUD restructured: big Press Start 2P #timer (clamp 32→64 px) at top centre with urgency colours — cyan above 10 s, amber 5–10 s, rose under 5 s with a step-blink animation. Below it a pip indicator 1 ✦ 2 ✦ 3 with the active stage glowing amber and completed stages in cyan. Score sits below the pips at a smaller size (was the giant top-centre line before)
  • Checkpoints rendered on the track — intermediate checkpoints (1 + 2) draw as a bright amber bar across the track at the target distance (dimmer when behind you); the chequered flag is a two-row alternating white + cyan pattern across the track at the final target
  • Finish screen — drawFinishOverlay() renders "FINISH!" plus a score breakdown (SCORE / + TIME BONUS / = total) over a fading backdrop. Holds for 6 seconds then commitReset() restarts at stage 1
  • One-liner added to the start overlay: "3 STAGES · BEAT THE CLOCK TO EACH CHECKPOINT · FLAG = TIME BONUS"
  • commitReset() calls initRace() so a fresh run always starts at stage 1 with a full clock
