CHANGES

  • New car.slipAngle field — radians offset between the car's heading and its actual motion direction. Translate now uses motionDir = heading + slipAngle instead of plain heading, so the world scrolls diagonally during a slide
  • Drift trigger: brake held + steer commitment over DRIFT_STEER_MIN = 0.18 + speed over DRIFT_MIN_SPEED = 220. Below the threshold the brake is just a brake (no slide)
  • Yaw boost while drifting (DRIFT_YAW_BOOST = 1.55) — heading rotates faster than motion. Slip angle eases toward ±DRIFT_MAX_SLIP = 0.50 rad (~29°) opposite the steer at 1/τ = 5.5. When the trigger releases, slip decays back to zero at 1/τ = 6.5 — grip recovers
  • Speed dynamics: brake during drift is softened (DRIFT_BRAKE_MUL = 0.30) so the slide actually continues, plus DRIFT_DRAG = 90 u/s² of bleed so it isn't free speed
  • Visual cue: wheels brighten to hot orange (#ff8d3a) and thicken (1.4 → 1.8 px) while |slipAngle| > 0.06 rad. The diagonal world-scroll already shows the slide; the brighter wheels just emphasise it
  • commitReset() clears slipAngle alongside the rest of the car state
